# Region Guide: Malibu & Calabasas (Priority 1)

## Overview

The Malibu and Calabasas corridor represents a primary target market for Neon Blonde due to high venue density, affluent demographic profile, appreciation for retro/dance/rock party bands, and strong private/outdoor event budgets.

## Target Cities & Areas

- **Calabasas / Old Town Calabasas**
- **Agoura Hills / Westlake Village border**
- **Malibu (PCH coastal corridor, Malibu Country Mart, Malibu Canyon)**

## Key Venues & Targets

### Major Clubs & Music Rooms
- **The Canyon Club (Agoura Hills / Calabasas border)**
  - *Capacity*: Large (~1,000)
  - *Format*: Full ticketed live band venue with premier sound & lighting.
  - *Fits*: High-energy 80s tribute / dance sets opening for national acts or headlining retro weekends.
- **Sagebrush Cantina (Calabasas)**
  - *Capacity*: 300+ outdoor patio & dance floor.
  - *Format*: Weekend afternoon and evening live party band entertainment. High demand for energetic 80s/pop-rock cover bands.

### Coastal & Garden / Tasting Venues
- **Duke's Malibu (Barefoot Bar)**
  - *Format*: Oceanfront live music series and private event buyout room.
- **Malibu Wines & Beer Garden (West Hills / Malibu border)**
  - *Format*: Outdoor garden events, weekend gatherings, private bookings.
- **Geoffrey's / Moonshadows**
  - *Format*: Upscale private functions, corporate parties, and seasonal summer gatherings.

## Booking Cadence & Lead Times
- **Summer municipal & patio events**: Booked in January – March.
- **Clubs & Saloons**: Booked 60–90 days in advance.
- **Private events & country clubs**: Booked 3–6 months in advance.
