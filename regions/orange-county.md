# Region Guide: Orange County (Connecting South Corridor)

## Overview

Orange County connects the Greater Los Angeles corridor with San Diego. Known for affluent coastal communities, high-budget private clubs, harbor venues, and active amphitheater summer concert series.

## Target Cities & Areas

- **Coastal OC (Newport Beach, Huntington Beach, Laguna Beach, Dana Point, San Clemente)**
- **Central & North OC (Costa Mesa, Anaheim, Orange, Fullerton, Irvine)**
- **South OC (Mission Viejo, San Juan Capistrano, Lake Forest)**

## Key Venues & Targets

### Major Clubs & Stages
- **The Coach House (San Juan Capistrano)**: Legendary 480-seat dinner and concert venue with deep history of touring tribute/retro acts.
- **Wayfarer (Costa Mesa)**: Vibrant indie and live music room.
- **SeaLegs at the Beach (Huntington Beach)**: Premier outdoor beachfront stage and live music series.
- **The Cave (Garden Grove / Anaheim)**: Live music stages.

### Municipal & Private Clubs
- **Newport Dunes Summer Concerts (Newport Beach)**
- **Huntington Beach Concerts in the Park / Pier Plaza**
- **Balboa Yacht Club / Newport Harbor Yacht Club**: Private member events.
