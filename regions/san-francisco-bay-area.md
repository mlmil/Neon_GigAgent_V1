# Region Guide: San Francisco & Bay Area (North Reach Corridor)

## Overview

San Francisco and the Greater Bay Area anchor the northern extent of Neon Blonde's California reach (spanning San Francisco to San Diego). The market features legendary rock ballrooms, wine country festival stages, and upscale corporate/private events.

## Target Cities & Areas

- **San Francisco (North Beach, SoMa, Mission, Marina)**
- **East Bay (Oakland, Berkeley, Livermore, Walnut Creek)**
- **North Bay / Marin (San Rafael, Mill Valley, Novato, Napa, Sonoma)**
- **South Bay / Peninsula (San Jose, Palo Alto, Redwood City, Santa Cruz)**

## Key Venues & Targets

### Historic Live Music Clubs & Theatres
- **Bimbo's 365 Club (San Francisco)**: Classic art-deco supper club and 685-capacity live music venue; ideal for premium retro/dance nights and corporate buyouts.
- **Great American Music Hall (San Francisco)**: Historic ornate concert hall.
- **Sweetwater Music Hall (Mill Valley)**: Renowned Marin County music showcase.
- **The Catalyst (Santa Cruz)**: Historic coastal live music club with loyal live music demographic.

### Wine Country & Festivals
- **Blue Note Napa (Napa)**: Downtown jazz/pop/rock supper club and summer festival stage.
- **Napa Valley & Sonoma Winery Summer Series**: Private and ticketed winery concerts.

## Booking Cadence & Logistics
- **Routing**: Scheduled as multi-day Northern California destination runs or high-budget private corporate galas.
- **Lead Times**: 90–180 days advance booking required.
