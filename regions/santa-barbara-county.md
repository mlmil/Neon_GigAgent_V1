# Region Guide: Santa Barbara County (Priority 1)

## Overview

Santa Barbara County offers premier music clubs, downtown winery barrel rooms, high-end private clubs, and active community festivals with strong budgets for experienced six-piece party/dance bands.

## Target Cities & Areas

- **Santa Barbara (State Street, Funk Zone, Waterfront)**
- **Goleta**
- **Carpinteria**
- **Santa Ynez Valley (Solvang, Santa Ynez, Los Olivos)**

## Key Venues & Targets

### Music Clubs & Theatres
- **SOhO Restaurant & Music Club (Santa Barbara)**: Premier dedicated live music venue with full sound engineering, green room, and active dancing crowd.
- **The Red Piano (Santa Barbara)**: State Street music bar with high weekend traffic.

### Wineries & Funk Zone
- **Carr Winery / Barrel Room (Santa Barbara)**: Regular band nights and private bookings.
- **Topa Topa Brewing Co. (Santa Barbara / Carpinteria)**: Weekend outdoor patio music.
- **M.Special Brewing Company (Goleta / State St)**: Established live music calendar.

### Municipal & Private Clubs
- **Santa Barbara Parks & Rec Concerts in the Park (Chase Palm Park)**
- **Santa Barbara Yacht Club / Montecito Country Club**: Private member events and parties.
