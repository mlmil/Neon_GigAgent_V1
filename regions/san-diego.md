# Region Guide: San Diego County (South Reach Corridor)

## Overview

San Diego County forms the southern anchor of Neon Blonde's performance reach (spanning San Francisco to San Diego). The region features a thriving beach-town music scene, historic theater stages, seaside resort venues, and high-energy nightlife districts.

## Target Cities & Areas

- **San Diego (Downtown / Gaslamp Quarter, Pacific Beach, Ocean Beach, North Park)**
- **North County Coastal (Encinitas, Solana Beach, Del Mar, Carlsbad, Oceanside)**
- **La Jolla & Coronado**

## Key Venues & Targets

### Historic Live Music Clubs & Theatres
- **Belly Up Tavern (Solana Beach)**: Iconic, world-class 600-capacity live music venue with top-tier production; regular destination for premier tribute and party bands.
- **The Casbah (San Diego / Midtown)**: Celebrated rock club.
- **Music Box (Little Italy / Downtown)**: 700-capacity premier music lounge with full concert sound.
- **Winstons Beach Club (Ocean Beach)**: Long-running community live music room with dedicated cover/party band following.

### Coastal, Resort & Outdoor
- **Humphreys Concerts by the Bay (San Diego / Shelter Island)**: Waterfront concert and lounge venue.
- **Tidal / Paradise Point Resort (Mission Bay)**: Resort events and summer music series.
- **Carlsbad TGIF Concerts in the Parks**: Major North County municipal summer concert series.
- **Encinitas Classic Car Nights / Street Fairs**: High-attendance coastal festivals.

## Booking Cadence & Logistics
- **Distance / Routing**: Best booked as weekend mini-tours, double-headers (e.g. Friday in OC / Saturday in SD), or high-yield private/corporate events.
- **Club Lead Times**: 90–120 days in advance for premier venues (Belly Up, Music Box).
