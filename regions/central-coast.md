# Region Guide: Central Coast (Priority 3)

## Overview

Central Coast (San Luis Obispo County and Northern Santa Barbara County) is an overflow / destination market. Suitable for weekend mini-tours, festival bookings, and high-budget winery / resort engagements.

## Target Areas

- **San Luis Obispo (SLO Downtown, Madonna Inn Expo)**
- **Pismo Beach / Avila Beach**
- **Paso Robles / Templeton (Wineries, Downtown Park)**
- **Morro Bay / Los Osos**

## Key Venues & Targets

- **Fremont Theater (SLO)**: Ticketed theater shows.
- **SLO Brew Live / The Rock (San Luis Obispo)**: Large event venue and brewery stage.
- **Tooth & Nail Winery / Castle (Paso Robles)**: High-production winery concerts.
- **Avila Beach Golf Resort**: Summer concert series.
- **Pismo Beach Pier Plaza / Summer Concerts**: Municipal summer series.
