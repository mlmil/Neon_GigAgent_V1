# Region Guide: San Fernando Valley & Greater LA (Priority 2)

## Overview

The San Fernando Valley and select North LA County areas represent targeted secondary markets. Due to traffic, logistics, and competition, prospecting is focused on high-yield, reputable rooms and private events rather than dense urban club routing.

## Target Areas

- **San Fernando Valley (Woodland Hills, Tarzana, Encino, Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Burbank)**
- **Santa Clarita Valley (Valencia, Newhall)**
- **West LA / Coastal LA (Secondary)**

## Key Venues & Targets

- **The Maui Sugar Mill Saloon (Tarzana)**: Live rock/blues/roots music institution.
- **The Baked Potato (Studio City)**: Specialized/intimate music room (specific acoustic/style fit).
- **818 Brewing / Local Breweries**: Weekend taproom entertainment.
- **Santa Clarita Concerts in the Park (Central Park)**: High-attendance summer festival stages.
