# Region Guide: Ventura County (Priority 1)

## Overview

Ventura County is the core home territory for Neon Blonde. High density of coastal bars, harbor venues, breweries, street fairs, and municipal concert series.

## Target Cities & Areas

- **Ventura (Downtown Main St, Ventura Harbor, Midtown)**
- **Oxnard (Channel Islands Harbor, Collection at RiverPark)**
- **Camarillo (Old Town, Community Center Park)**
- **Thousand Oaks / Westlake Village**
- **Simi Valley / Moorpark**

## Key Venues & Targets

### Harbor & Coastal
- **The Lookout Bar & Grill (Oxnard / Channel Islands Harbor)**: Established live cover band weekends.
- **The Greek Mediterranean Steak & Seafood (Ventura Harbor)**: Dancing, weekend bands, harbor views.
- **Ventura Beach House / Harbor Cove Cafe**: Summer outdoor music series.

### Downtown & Nightlife
- **Winchester's Grill & Saloon (Ventura Downtown)**: High-foot-traffic patio & saloon.
- **The Six Chow House (Westlake Village)**: Boutique weekend live acts.
- **Tarantula Hill Brewing Co. (Thousand Oaks)**: Large patio space and community events.

### Municipal & Festivals
- **Camarillo Concerts in the Park (Pleasant Valley Rec & Park)**
- **Thousand Oaks Summer Concert Series (Conejo Rec & Park)**
- **Ventura County Fair / Harbor Festivals**
